1.2 Supported functions and scope

From the currently allowed Main Controller evidence, the pilot should retain these distinct local sub-functions:

Configuration update
Captures channel, mode, timing, sampling, storage and debug settings before operation.
Interacts with Device FSM, Elab FSM, ADC phase generation and result-routing selection.

Device FSM
Controls the device lifecycle across Boot, Idle, startup, Operative, Sleep and Error states.
Interacts with ADSP boot completion, power/clock controls, Elab FSM and status/error outputs.

Elab FSM
Selects and sequences the ECG, BIA, GSR and PPG elaboration paths after operation starts.
Interacts with Device FSM, ADC phase generation, averaging, PPG sequencing and output routing.

ECG startup control
Executes the local ECG/health-channel startup and recovery sequence.
Interacts with Analog Domain controls, ECG clocks and Device FSM startup progression.

BIA startup and modulation control
Executes impedance-channel startup, current-injection, modulation and chopping control.
Interacts with Analog Domain, clock generation and Device FSM startup progression.

ADC phase generation and calibration
Controls ADC enable, readiness, sampling start, conversion completion and calibration flow.
Interacts with Elab FSM, Analog Domain ADC controls and sampled-data processing.

ECG/BIA acquisition sequencing
Applies the selected ECG/BIA acquisition sequence and ADC mux progression.
Interacts with Elab FSM, ADC phase generation, averaging and subsequent result routing.

GSR acquisition and CDS processing
Schedules GSR acquisitions, controls current-on/off behavior and supports CDS measurement handling.
Interacts with ADC acquisition, averaging and the Device FSM sleep/operative path.

PPG FSM and frame sequencing
Controls PPG startup, frame execution, RX selection, ADC sampling and frame repetition.
Interacts with Elab FSM, Analog Domain TX/RX controls, ADC and PPG status reporting.

PPG ALC and IOFF coordination
Coordinates ALC compensation, IOFF calibration, compensation completion and transition to PPG receive startup.
Interacts with the ALC function, PPG FSM, comparator/BTIA controls and ADC sampling.

Digital-ramp control
Controls TX preset plus rising and falling digital-ramp phases for PPG operation.
Interacts with PPG FSM and Analog Domain IDAC/TX reference controls.

Averaging and result formation
Coordinates ECG/BIA/GSR averaging and PPG raw/noise accumulation into local results.
Interacts with ADC conversion completion, PPG/GSR processing paths and data output selection.

Data routing, tagging and storage
Routes eligible results to Regmap-visible outputs or FIFO through the local AHB-master path; applies time-slot or frame tags where supported.
Interacts with averaging/elaboration completion, FIFO, Regmap and data-storage mode.
