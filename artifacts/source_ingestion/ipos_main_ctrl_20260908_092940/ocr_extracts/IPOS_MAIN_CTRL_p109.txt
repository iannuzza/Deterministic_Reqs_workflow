5.11.4. PPG Frame Repetition 
The frame repetition is a classic mean operation between two 20 bit PPG data. The result of the 
frame is accumulated on a 24 bit accumulator that is divided by the number of repeated frame. 
The number of repetition frames supported is 0 (no frame repetead), 2,4 and 8. 
5.11.4.1. Requirements 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_ 2145] REQUIREMENT : 
The Frame shall work as follows: 
1. Select how many times a particular frame shall be repeated 
2. The result of each repetition shall be obtained as explained in 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_1 40] REQUIREMENT  
3. After the last repetition has been accumulated, the data output shall be obtained dividing 
by the number of repeated frames the accumulated data to obtain a 20 bit data. 
The final result shall be computed as follows: 
 
Number of times the Frame has been repeated Result 
2 (Res_F_R1 + Res_F_R2)/2 
4 (Res_F_R1 + Res_F_R2+Res_F_R3+Res_F_R4)/4 
8 (Res_F_R1 + Res_F_R2+Res_F_R3+…..+Res_F_R8)/8
