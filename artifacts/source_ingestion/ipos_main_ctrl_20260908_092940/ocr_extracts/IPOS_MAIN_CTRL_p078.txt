5.6.3. Requirements 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_080]  REQUIREMENT : 
When the user selects an operative mode that includes the BIA channel, a start-up shall be done. 
[END] 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_081]  REQUIREMENT : 
When i_imp_en signal is equal to 1, the o_pd_imp_from_bia shall be go low. [END] 
 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_082]  REQUIREMENT : 
When i_imp_curinj signal is equal to 1, the o_imp_curinj_pd shall go low. On the other hand, if 
i_imp_curinj is equal to 0, the o_imp_curinj_pd shall stay high. [END] 
 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_083]  REQUIREMENT : 
At least 5ms after the falling of o_imp_curinj_pd and o_pd_imp_from_bia, if i_imp_curinj is equal 
to 1, o_en_ck_m_p_q shall be high and ck_m, ck_p and ck_q shall be generated from Clock 
generator block. On the other hand, if i_imp_curinj is equal to 0, the ck_m_p_q shall stay low.  
[END] 
 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_084]  REQUIREMENT : 
At least 15ms after the falling of o_pd_imp_from_bia, the o_rst_imp shall go low. [END] 
 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_085]  REQUIREMENT : 
20ms after the falling of o_rst_imp, if i_clk_imp_en is set to 1, the o_en_ck_chop_imp shall be high 
and ck_chop_imp shall be generated from Clock generator block. On the hand, if i_clk_imp_en is 
set to 0, the o_en_ck_chop_imp shall stay low. [END] 
 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_086]  REQUIREMENT : 
30ms after the falling of o_rst_imp, o_rsti_imp shall go low. [END] 
 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_087]  REQUIREMENT : 
If i_su_hlt_dly is set to 0,at least 205 ms after the falling of o_rsti_imp, o_sug_imp shall go low. 
Instead, if i_su_hlt_dly is set to 1, 755ms after the falling of o_rsti_imp, o_sug_imp shall go low.  
[END]
