When in SECOND_ALC_SAMPLES state, the o_en_Txi shall be set low after the end at least of

[END] 
 
[IPOS_STBIO1_MAIN_CONTROLLER_113]  REQUIREMENT : 
When in SECOND_ALC_SAMPLES state, the PPG_FSM shall stay in this state until the rise of 
the end_of_conversion signal from the ADC block. After that, if there are no more frames to run, 
the PPG_FSM shall go into the Reset State, otherwise, if there are other frames to run and channel 
rx is not changed, the PPG_FSM shall go into the Rx_start_up phase. On the other hand, if there 
are other frame to run and rx_channel is changed, the PPG_FSM shall go into the RESET_PPG 
state and the into the ALC_Compensation  state. [END]
