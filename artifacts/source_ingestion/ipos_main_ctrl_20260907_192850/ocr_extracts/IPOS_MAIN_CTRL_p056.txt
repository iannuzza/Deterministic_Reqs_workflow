5.3.2. Description of the Block 
This block is divided into two FSMs. The first FSM called Device_FSM, is in charge of identify the 
working state of the device. The second FSM called Elab_FSM, is in charge of identify what signal 
is in elaboration and to check and generate signal from and for the Analog Domain. The Elab_FSM 
is also in charge to start writing to the AHB Crossbar and to talk with Hardware DSPs block like 
the Avarage Circuit and ADSP, or to start PPG operation talking with the PPG_FSM. This Block is 
also in charge to turn of the Fast Ring Oscillator (16 MHz) and to enable the User Read Operation.
